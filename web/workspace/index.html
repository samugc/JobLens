      Technical details of the analysis

      This shows only what the reader returned. It does not verify that a CV is true, complete, or suitable for a role.

      • Pages and paragraphs come from the reader, unchanged.
      • A read document may still have warnings or incomplete text.
      • A failed document says nothing bad about the person who wrote it.
      • Nothing is stored: refreshing loses the session.
